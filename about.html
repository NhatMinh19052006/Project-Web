About SkyPath

Zero Traffic Zero Emissions

Who We Are

SkyPath provides aerial logistics solutions for e-commerce businesses. Our 24/7 automated fleet removes geographical barriers, reduces shipping costs and delivers parcels without adding to road congestion.

Our Mission

To make delivery fast, affordable and sustainable by powering our fleet with solar technology and protecting the environment for the next generation.

Our Values

  • Reliability Businesses trust us to deliver on time, every time.
  • Sustainability Solar-powered technology keeps our emissions at zero.
  • Innovation We keep improving our automated fleet and routes.
  • Accessibility We aim to reach places that roads cannot.
